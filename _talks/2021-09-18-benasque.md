The [TAE (Taller de Altas Energías) - Workshop on High Energy Physics](https://www.benasque.org/2021tae/) is an international workshop aimed at completing the education of first and second year graduated students who are starting their research on experimental or theoretical High Energy Physics, Astroparticles and Cosmology. I attended the workshop in September 2021 and gave a student presentation on the Analysis of first data from LST-1 of the next-generation ground-based gamma-ray observatory, Cherenkov Telescope Array Observatory. This was based on my PhD work until then.
