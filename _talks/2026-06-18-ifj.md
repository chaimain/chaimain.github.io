IFJ PAN frequently organizes Seminarium dla młodych (Seminar for Young People), for young scientists (PhD students to employees without habilitation) from its different divisions. Some of the main goals of this series of meetings is to mutually improve presentation skills, learning about each other's interests and passions, and discussing challenges and difficulties in conducting research. In [June 2026](https://indico.ifj.edu.pl/event/1793/), I presented an overview of the different searches made by using nearly two decades of the Pierre Auger Observatory. I also highlighted the ongoing work in our group on direction-time clustering analysis, where we use Boosted Decision Tree methods to improve the photon-hadron discrimination, and perform a preliminary target search for possible ultra-high-energy photon emissions.
