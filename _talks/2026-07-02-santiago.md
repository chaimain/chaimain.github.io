The European Cosmic Ray Symposium (ECRS) is held every two years in one of the European countries. The main goal of the Symposia is to gather European Physics community performing research of cosmic rays and related fields, to present recent results, newly emerging ideas and forthcoming experiments. I participated in the [29th edition of the Symposia](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1536846/overview) in the last week of June and first week of July 2026 and presented the usage of a modified clustering algorithm in the search of ultra-high-energy photon sources, from a list of assumed "PeVatron" sources.
