[IFJ PAN Particle Physics Summer Student Programme](https://ppss.ifj.edu.pl/index.php) is aimed to introduce the students of second or higher year of physics and similar fields from Poland and abroad, to particle physics. The program consists of 1 week of lectures and 3 weeks of project, under the supervision of a tutor. I was the tutor to 2 students in July 2025, on the topic of "Developing Python tools to search for ultra-high-energy photons using clustering algorithms", which used the [uhecluster](https://gitlab.com/uhecluster/uhecluster) package.
